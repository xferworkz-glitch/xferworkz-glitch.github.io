Monocytes (Absolute) Mono

LOINC 1025-2
Unit: ×10⁹/L
Adult0.02 – 0.1 ×10⁹/L

Platelets & Coagulation

5 tests

Prothrombin Time PT

LOINC 6349-8
Unit: seconds
Adult11 – 13.5 sec
Extrinsic + common pathway. Affected by warfarin, vitamin K deficiency, liver disease, DIC, factor VII deficiency.

Prothrombin Time PT

LOINC 6349-8
Unit: INR
INR (therapeutic warfarin)2.0 – 3.0 INR
Target depends on indication: mechanical mitral valve ~ 2.5–3.5; most others 2.0–3.0.

Activated Partial Thromboplastin Time aPTT

LOINC 6426-1
Unit: seconds
Adult25 – 35 sec
Intrinsic + common pathway. Prolonged: heparin, hemophilia A/B, vWD (severe), lupus anticoagulant, DIC, factor deficiencies.

Fibrinogen (Clauss) Fib / Fbg

LOINC 2438-7
Unit: g/L (mg/dL)
Adult2.0 – 4.0 g/L (200 – 400 mg/dL)
Acute phase reactant — elevated in inflammation. ↓ in DIC, massive hemorrhage, liver failure, dilutional. Critical: < 1.0 g/L (bleeding risk).

D-Dimer D-Dimer

LOINC 47222-7
Unit: ng/mL (or µg/L FEU)
Typical cutoff< 500 ng/mL (< 0.5 µg/mL FEU)
Negative D-dimer helps exclude acute VTE in low-probability patients. Elevated in thrombosis, inflammation, infection, malignancy, pregnancy, post-surgery, liver disease, age. Age-adjusted cutoffs (age × 10 ng/mL) used in some settings.

Thrombin Time TT

LOINC 74090-0
Unit: seconds
Adult14 – 22 sec
Prolonged: hypofibrinogenemia, dysfibrinogenemia, heparin, fibrinogen degradation products, some inhibitors. Used in fibrinogen assessment and heparin monitoring.

Electrolytes

7 tests

Sodium Na

LOINC 2951-2
Unit: mmol/L
Adult135 – 145 mmol/L
Hyponatremia < 135 mmol/L; severity of symptoms depends on rate of change. Hypernatremia > 145 mmol/L (usually water deficit).

Sodium Na

LOINC 2951-2
Unit: mmol/L
Critical low< 120 mmol/L
Severe hyponatremia — risk of cerebral oedema, seizures. Correct slowly (max ~8 mmol/L/24h) to avoid osmotic demyelination.

Sodium Na

LOINC 2951-2
Unit: mmol/L
Critical high> 160 mmol/L
Severe hypernatremia — neurological risk; requires careful free-water replacement.

Potassium K

LOINC 2823-3
Unit: mmol/L (mEq/L)
Adult3.5 – 5.0 mmol/L
Hypokalemia < 3.5 mmol/L; severe < 2.5 mmol/L (rhythm risk). Hyperkalemia > 5.0 mmol/L; ECG changes / arrhythmia risk > 6.0–6.5 mmol/L. Pseudohyperkalemia from haemolysis common.

Potassium K

LOINC 2823-3
Unit: mmol/L
Critical low< 2.5 mmol/L
Risk of arrhythmia, muscle weakness, ileus. Requires urgent correction.

Potassium K

LOINC 2823-3
Unit: mmol/L
Critical high> 6.5 mmol/L
Requires urgent management; ECG monitoring; consider calcium, insulin/glucose, dialysis.

Chloride Cl

LOINC 2075-0
Unit: mmol/L
Adult96 – 106 mmol/L
Disturbed in acid-base disorders — high in hyperchloremic metabolic acidosis; low in vomiting/NG losses, diuretics. Often interpreted with anion gap.

Bicarbonate (Total CO₂) HCO₃ / CO₂

LOINC 2028-9
Unit: mmol/L
Adult22 – 28 mmol/L
Reflects metabolic component of acid-base. Low: metabolic acidosis (DKA, lactic acidosis, renal failure, diarrhoea). High: metabolic alkalosis (vomiting, diuretics).

Total Calcium Ca

LOINC 1789-1
Unit: mmol/L (mg/dL)
Adult2.15 – 2.55 mmol/L (8.5 – 10.2 mg/dL)
Corrected calcium ≈ total Ca + 0.02 × (40 − albumin) when albumin abnormal. Ionized calcium is more physiologically relevant in critical illness.

Ionized Calcium Ca²⁺

LOINC 1789-1
Unit: mmol/L (mg/dL)
Adult1.10 – 1.30 mmol/L (4.4 – 5.2 mg/dL)
Preferred in critically ill, hypoalbuminemia, citrate exposure (transfusion, dialysis).

Magnesium Mg

LOINC 2452-9
Unit: mmol/L (mg/dL)
Adult0.70 – 1.05 mmol/L (1.7 – 2.6 mg/dL)
Hypomagnesemia < 0.70 mmol/L — arrhythmia risk (especially with hypokalemia/hypocalcemia), seizures, neuromuscular irritability. Often co-depleted with K and Ca.

Magnesium Mg

LOINC 2452-9
Unit: mmol/L
Critical low< 0.50 mmol/L
Can cause torsades, severe arrhythmia.

Phosphate PO₄ / Phos

LOINC 14682-9
Unit: mmol/L (mg/dL)
Adult0.75 – 1.50 mmol/L (2.3 – 4.5 mg/dL)
Hypophosphatemia: refeeding syndrome, respiratory alkalosis, alcohol withdrawal, malabsorption. Hyperphosphatemia: renal failure, tumor lysis, rhabdomyolysis.

Phosphate PO₄ / Phos

LOINC 14682-9
Unit: mmol/L
Newborn / child1.25 – 2.50 mmol/L (4.0 – 7.8 mg/dL)
Higher in growing children (bone deposition).

Hyperuricemia > 7.0 mg/dL (men), > 6.0 mg/dL (women) — gout risk, uric acid nephrolithiasis. Also elevated in tumor lysis, renal failure, diuretics.

Uric Acid UA

Negative acute phase reactant — falls in inflammation, infection, malignancy. Also in liver synthetic failure, nephrotic syndrome, malabsorption, protein-losing enteropathy, severe burns.

Cardiac Markers

6 tests

Troponin T (High-Sensitivity) hs-cTnT

LOINC 26508-4
Unit: ng/L (or pg/mL)
99th percentile (typical)< 14 ng/L (varies by assay)
High-sensitivity troponin T — assay-specific 99th percentile. Elevated in myocardial necrosis (NSTEMI/STEMI, myocarditis, PE with RV strain, severe HF, renal failure (chronic elevation), tachycardia-related/demand ischaemia).

Troponin T (High-Sensitivity) hs-cTnT

LOINC 26508-4
Unit: ng/L
Serial rise/fallSignificant change over 1–3 h
Diagnosis of acute MI requires a rise and/or fall in troponin with at least one value above 99th percentile + evidence of ischaemia (symptoms, ECG changes, imaging).

Troponin I (High-Sensitivity) hs-cTnI

LOINC 26508-4
Unit: ng/L (pg/mL)
99th percentile (typical)< 30 ng/L (assay-dependent)
hs-cTnI — assay-specific cutoffs differ; always use local laboratory's reference. Interpretation principles same as troponin T.

Creatine Kinase MB Isoenzyme CK-MB

LOINC 2092-3
Unit: ng/mL (mass) / U/L (activity)
Total CK-MB (mass)0 – 5 ng/mL (or < 25 U/L activity)
Less specific than troponin — also elevated in skeletal muscle injury. CK-MB relative index (% of total CK) helps distinguish cardiac vs skeletal source. Rises 4–6h post-MI, peaks ~24h, normalizes ~48–72h.

Lactate Dehydrogenase LDH

LOINC 2524-7
Unit: U/L (IU/L)
Adult120 – 250 U/L
Non-specific marker of tissue injury. Elevated in MI (historical), haemolysis, liver disease, malignancy (e.g. lymphoma, some germ-cell tumors with LDH as marker), megaloblastic anaemia, muscle injury. LDH isoenzymes may localize source.

Brain Natriuretic Peptide BNP

LOINC 30843-8
Unit: pg/mL (pmol/L)
Normal< 100 pg/mL (< ~28 pmol/L)
BNP elevated in heart failure — reflects ventricular wall stress. Levels correlate with severity. Also elevated in renal failure, PE, advanced age, tachycardia. Cutoffs vary by assay and clinical context.

BNP BNP

LOINC 30843-8
Unit: pg/mL
Acute HF rule-out (typical)< 100 pg/mL
Low BNP in acute setting helps exclude decompensated HF as cause of dyspnoea (assay/context specific).

N-Terminal Pro-BNP NT-proBNP

LOINC 30843-8
Unit: pg/mL
Age-adjusted cutoffs< 450 (< 50 yr) · < 900 (50–75 yr) · < 1800 (> 75 yr) pg/mL
NT-proBNP has longer half-life than BNP; elevated in HF. Renal failure raises NT-proBNP independently. Not for BNP-guided therapy titration in the same direct way due to longer half-life.

Lipid Panel

4 tests

Total Cholesterol TC

LOINC 2093-3
Unit: mg/dL (mmol/L)
Desirable< 200 mg/dL (< 5.2 mmol/L)
Borderline high: 200–239 mg/dL. High: ≥ 240 mg/dL. Goal depends on overall cardiovascular risk and guidelines (ATP III, ACC/AHA, ESC/EAS, local).

LDL Cholesterol LDL-C

LOINC 2093-3
Unit: mg/dL (mmol/L)
Optimal< 100 mg/dL (< 2.6 mmol/L)
Near-optimal: 100–129. Borderline high: 130–159. High: 160–189. Very high: ≥ 190 mg/dL. Higher-risk patients (diabetes, established CVD, FH) have lower targets (e.g. < 70 mg/dL, < 55 mg/dL for very high risk per ESC).

HDL Cholesterol HDL-C

LOINC 2085-9
Unit: mg/dL (mmol/L)
Low (risk factor) — men< 40 mg/dL (< 1.0 mmol/L)
Low HDL is an independent cardiovascular risk factor. High HDL: ≥ 60 mg/dL considered protective (historically).

HDL Cholesterol HDL-C

LOINC 2085-9
Unit: mg/dL
Low (risk factor) — women< 50 mg/dL (< 1.3 mmol/L)
Women typically have higher HDL than men.

Triglycerides TG

LOINC 2073-6
Unit: mg/dL (mmol/L)
Normal< 150 mg/dL (< 1.7 mmol/L)
Borderline high: 150–199. High: 200–499. Very high: ≥ 500 mg/dL (risk of pancreatitis). Elevated in diabetes (poor control), obesity, alcohol, hypothyroidism, renal disease, medications (estrogen, glucocorticoids, retinoids), familial hypertriglyceridemia.

Triglycerides TG

LOINC 2073-6
Unit: mg/dL
Very high / pancreatitis risk≥ 500 mg/dL
Consider fibrate/insulin/heparin avoidance depending on context; address urgently to prevent acute pancreatitis.

Glucose & Diabetes

3 tests

LOINC 2345-7
Unit: mg/dL (mmol/L)
Normal fasting70 – 99 mg/dL (3.9 – 5.5 mmol/L)
Fasting ≥ 8h. Impaired fasting glucose (pre-diabetes): 100–125 mg/dL (5.6–6.9 mmol/L). Diabetes: ≥ 126 mg/dL (7.0 mmol/L) on two separate tests (or one with classic symptoms).

Glucose (Fasting) Glucose / FPG
